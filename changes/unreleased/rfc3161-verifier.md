Section: Added

New package `audit/rfc3161`: a strict, standard-library-only verifier for RFC 3161 time-stamp tokens, the independent time source that the approver identity design requires for gates above the top tier; it reads one narrow DER profile (one `SignerInfo`, SHA-256 or better, ECDSA P-256/P-384, RSA of 3072 bits or more, or Ed25519, the `contentType`, `messageDigest` and `signingCertificateV2` signed attributes, a critical time-stamping-only TSA certificate chained to the auditor's anchors at `genTime`) and refuses everything else with a named `Reason`, checked against OpenSSL `ts` and DigiCert tokens, differentially against `openssl ts -verify`, with a negative vector per refusal, a fuzz target and a fixed mutation budget; it is not called by any command or gate yet, and a FreeTSA token is refused because it identifies its signer by the SHA-1 `signingCertificate` attribute only.
