The gate-side records of the planned approver identity feature (build step 1; certificate-bound approvers stay refused at gate configuration, `ErrConfig`, until the last change of the feature, so nothing reaches a bare-key policy). A gate with a certificate-bound approver anchors first: a decision that verifies but has no covering signed head makes the gate ask the store to publish one (`Reanchorer`, at most once per 10 s per run), and "no covering head" is reported only for a decision whose chain, identifier, scheme and signature already verify, so a junk decision never makes the gate sign. It writes phase notes (`awaiting-anchor`, `awaiting-status`) and journals, before the tally, the CRLs the count rests on by hash (`approval-status:<sha256>`, a CRL above 256 KiB goes to `CertApprover.StatusArchive`) and an `approval-identity:<id>:<generation>` record: the policy in force with every verdict-affecting parameter, the roster digest, and for each counted decision its chain hashes, anchor, covering head, time source, tier, basis and status hashes, bound to the tally by a digest of its stored bytes. `StatusParams.Validate` and `CertApprover.ValidateConfig` (including `CAPeriod <= ExecStatusAge/2` for the leaf's issuer and a bounded status snapshot or an archive) run when the gate is configured.
