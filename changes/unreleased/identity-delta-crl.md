Section: Added

Delta CRL merging for the planned certificate-bound approver identity status rule (build step 1, not yet used by the approval gate, so no behaviour changes). `StatusSnapshot.AddCRL` accepts a delta CRL with a critical DER INTEGER delta indicator; evaluating a chain pairs each delta with the highest qualifying base (RFC 5280 sections 5.2.4 and 6.3.3), reads the merged view as of the delta with the earlier of the two nextUpdate values, treats numbers as one sequence across bases and deltas, reads the listings of every delta that verifies (an unpaired delta gives no evidence of absence), and treats holds as unaccepted by default (a certificate that was ever on hold is refused unless `StatusParams.AllowHolds`, which then refuses a decision only inside the hold). OpenSSL-written certificates and CRLs, including overlapping issuance and refusals on real output, are checked in as conformance fixtures (`audit/testdata/openssl`, `gen.sh` regenerates them).
