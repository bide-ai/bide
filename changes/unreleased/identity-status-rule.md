Section: Added

`audit.StatusSnapshot` and `CertIdentity.EvaluateStatus`: the revocation half of the planned certificate-bound approver identity (build step 1, not yet used by the approval gate, so no behaviour changes). A snapshot reads CRLs and refuses what bide cannot read without guessing (issuing distribution points, indirect CRLs, critical extensions it does not process, SHA-1 signatures, a missing CRL number or nextUpdate, delta CRLs for now); evaluating an accepted chain checks every non-anchor certificate against CRLs that verify under its own issuing CA, computes the effective revocation time (the earlier of the invalidity date and the revocation time, retroactive to notBefore for a compromise with no invalidity date, with an optional grace for routine reasons), treats a listing in any CRL of the series as a revocation from its time on, reads absence only from CRLs issued after the decision and no later than the first CRL beyond notAfter, ignores status signed by a distrusted key, and reports a tier (no information, provisional, final: status reaching A plus the window over time coverage from the CRL in force at A, with L(C) needed only once the status falls after notAfter) with the window, the CRLs relied on and the caveat that later reports are not covered. New `agent.ReasonRevoked`, `ReasonStatusUnknown` and `ReasonStatusStale`.
