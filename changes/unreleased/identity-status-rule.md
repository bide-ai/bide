Section: Added

`audit.StatusSnapshot` and `CertIdentity.EvaluateStatus`: the revocation half of the planned certificate-bound approver identity (build step 1, not yet used by the approval gate, so no behaviour changes). A snapshot reads CRLs and refuses what bide cannot read without guessing (issuing distribution points, indirect CRLs, critical extensions it does not process, SHA-1 signatures, a missing CRL number or nextUpdate, duplicate extensions, delta CRLs for now); a snapshot can be bound to its issuers (the signature is checked before the entries are built), is size-limited and evicts its oldest CRLs; evaluating an accepted chain checks every non-anchor certificate against CRLs that verify under its own issuing CA, computes the effective revocation time (the earlier of the invalidity date and the revocation time, retroactive to notBefore for a compromise with no invalidity date, with an optional grace for routine reasons), treats a listing in any CRL of the series as a revocation from its time on, reads absence only from CRLs issued after the decision and no later than the first CRL beyond notAfter, ignores status signed by a distrusted key, and reports a tier (no information, provisional, final: status reaching A plus the window over time coverage from the CRL in force at A, with L(C) needed only once the status falls after notAfter, and coverage shown by the CA's declared `CAPeriod` rather than by nextUpdate) with the window, the CRLs relied on and the caveat that later reports are not covered. New `agent.ReasonRevoked`, `ReasonStatusUnknown` and `ReasonStatusStale`. A finding is verified only when `Final()`; its `Basis` says what it rests on, and a live count (`CountLive`, never implied by `Now`) is provisional at most. A snapshot can be unbounded for audits and counts its evictions.
