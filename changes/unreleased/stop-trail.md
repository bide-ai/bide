The kill switch's audit trail: an audited `agent.StopControl` given `WithStopTrail(audit.NewStopTrail(events))` writes every signed record (a stop, a lift, a policy transition, the root, a mode change) to a stop trail of salted event leaves that an `AuditedEventStore` signs and anchors, in an order that keeps the evidence at least as strong as the effect (a stop is in force first and evidenced second; every other record is evidenced first), reports a failed trail write as `*agent.StopTrailError`, and `StopControl.Reaudit` appends what the trail lacks. `bide audit stops` verifies a trail offline (`-trail`, or `-db` and `-postgres-env`) against the pinned policy (`-stop-policy`) and the chain of signed transitions from its root, against signed heads (`-sth`, `-pubkey`) so a deleted or reordered record is found, and, with a store, against the control run record for record. `audit.VerifyStops`, `audit.ExportStopTrail`, `audit.SignStopTrail` and `agent.VerifyStopRecords` are the library side. A trail record that never reached the control run (a crash between the two writes) is listed as unapplied, and `StopControl.ApplyPending` applies it; an unattested tail fails by default and `-max-unattested` relaxes it. Guide: docs/guides/kill-switch.md.
