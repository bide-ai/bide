Section: Added

`agent.RaisesSafety(reason)` (experimental, with `ToolSpec.RaisesSafety`): `agent.New` now refuses with `ErrConfig` a wrapper whose declared `Safety` is safer than a tool it embeds or unwraps, at any depth (the order is a side effect, then `Idempotent`, then `ReadOnly`), naming the wrapper, the tool and both levels; a wrapper that really does make the call safer declares `RaisesSafety(reason)` (the reason follows the rules of `agent.Trusted`). The reason is in the declarations document (`raises_safety`, and `idempotent` for a retry-safe side effect) and, only when set, in the declarations digest, so existing digests are unchanged. `bide doctor -declarations` lists it and `bide doctor -diff` reports adding it as WEAKENED and removing it as strengthened. Behavior change: an agent with such a wrapper (for example a `ReadOnly` wrapper over a side-effecting tool) now fails at `New` until the wrapper declares the inner tool's `Safety` or `RaisesSafety`. See docs/reference/extension-points.md and docs/guides/untrusted-content.md.
