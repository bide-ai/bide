Section: Added

`audit.IdentityPolicy` and `CertIdentity.VerifyChain`: the certificate half of the planned certificate-bound approver identity (build step 1, not yet used by the approval gate, so no behaviour changes). Given a decision's leaf-first chain, the time the approver signed and the time the decision became provable, it accepts exactly one chain from the policy's trust anchors through pinned intermediates, with the extended key usage nested along the chain, an algorithm and key profile (RSA 3072 or better, ECDSA P-256 or P-384, Ed25519, ML-DSA-65; never SHA-1), per-key `Distrust` dates, one subject alternative name of the configured kind (an opaque person URI, or an email only by explicit choice) in a strict canonical form, a required certificate policy, and a leaf key whose scheme is derived from the certificate. Failures are `*audit.CertError` carrying one of the new `agent.ReasonNoCert`, `ReasonCertChain`, `ReasonCertIdentity`, `ReasonCertUsage` or `ReasonCertWindow` and the precise cause. Revocation status, the v4 decision bytes and the gate wiring follow in later changes.
