Section: Changed

`signer/pkcs11`: `Config.RequireNonExtractable` now also refuses a key whose token reports `CKA_NEVER_EXTRACTABLE` or `CKA_ALWAYS_SENSITIVE` as false (`ErrExtractable`), since a key that was extractable once, or not sensitive, may have been copied out before it was marked otherwise; a token that does not report these two attributes is taken at its word on `CKA_EXTRACTABLE` and `CKA_SENSITIVE` as before; `Open` also refuses a PIN shorter than `MinPINLength` (4 characters, SoftHSM2's minimum) with `agent.ErrConfig`, so the PIN scrubbing of error messages cannot garble them. `Config.ProtectedAuthPath` logs in with no PIN on a token with a PIN pad (`CKF_PROTECTED_AUTHENTICATION_PATH`), is refused with `ErrNoProtectedAuthPath` on a token without one, and is exclusive with `Config.PIN`. A key with `CKA_ALWAYS_AUTHENTICATE` is now signed with: the signer logs in as the context-specific user between `C_SignInit` and `C_Sign` with the same PIN source (it failed before), and `Open` skips its self-test signature for such a key.
