How a gate with a certificate-bound approver names its tally and asks again, in the planned approver identity feature (no run writes an exec hold yet). Such a gate records its terminal tally under `approval-tally:<id>:ident` (`agent.ApprovalIdentTallyStep`) and treats a plain-name tally that passed as superseded: the call does not run on it, `Run` returns `*ApprovalPending` with the new `agent.PendingIdentReapproval`, and the new count supersedes it. A valid exec hold of generation `g` (`agent.ExecHold`) supersedes the passed generation-`g` tally, so the gate has no outcome until it counts generation `g+1` under `approval-tally:<id>:ident-reask:<g+1>` (`agent.PendingExecHoldReapproval`); a hold over a denial, with no tally under it, or of another call, generation or format is ignored. `agent.RecordedTally`, the gate, `agent.EvaluateGate`, the notifier (new event key `ApprovalIdentReaskKey`, decided names, journal events), `agent.LatestPauseNote` and `ValidReaskNote` (re-ask notes `agent.ApprovalIdentReaskStep`), the deadline check, `plan` `Conform`, and `audit.ApprovalEvidence` and `VerifyApprovals` (which now carry the superseded tallies, the holds and the notes) read the chain by one rule. A gate of bare-key approvers is unchanged. A certificate-bound gate whose count passed without excluding the requester refuses with `ErrConfig` if the policy then excludes it.
