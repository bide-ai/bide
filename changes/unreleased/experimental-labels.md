The kill switch, redaction and legal holds are labelled experimental in the API reference and the stability page: every symbol of `agent` and `audit` declared for them (`StopControl`, `StopSwitch`, the stop records, policy and trail, `Redactor`, `Compactor`, `PlanRedaction`, `Redact`, the redaction trail, `Holder`, `PlaceLegalHold`, `LiftLegalHold` and the rest) now carries *(experimental)* through the label table's symbol rules (`internal/tools/apiref/labels.go`), and the kill switch and retention guides and `docs/trust/stability.md` say what it means: the Go API and the command flags may change in a minor release with a changelog entry, their records follow the pre-1.0 format policy of the stability page, the tombstone format and leaf hash stay core, and the stated guarantees (a stop in force holds the calls it covers, an erasure is accounted for in a signed trail) hold. No code behavior changes.
