`bide audit notifications` and `audit.VerifyNotifications` check offline that every event that needed a person was notified and acknowledged, and when. The notification trail is read strictly (an unknown record kind or field is an error, not skipped) and must be a history a notifier writes (an event observed once and first, nothing after its acknowledgement or clearing, an acknowledgement after a claim, in-order times); with a signed tree head over the trail (`audit.SignNotifyTrail`, new, and `audit.ExportNotifyTrail`, format `bide.audit.notify-trail-export.v1`) a deleted, reordered or changed record is found, and a trail with no head is reported `UNANCHORED` (the notifier does not sign its trail; `-unanchored` accepts one). Each event gets a verdict: `acked` with its times, attempts and authentication, `acked_late`, `pending`, `failing`, `overdue`, `cleared_before_notification`, `cleared_unacknowledged`, `never_notified`; the run journals are cross-checked (`agent.JournalNotifyEvents`, new) so an approval gate, unknown-outcome halt or aborted run with no notify record is `never_notified`, and an overdue record without its notice, a long recovery failure episode without its event and a clearing without its all-clear are caught the same way. A gate decided by m-of-n decisions whose all-clear is missing past `-grace` of the newest decision's `recorded_at` is `never_notified`; one whose resolution the journal does not time is the warning `all_clear_untimed`, like `unobserved_resolved` (a decided gate no notifier observed), and `-strict` (library: `Strict`) fails the audit on either. The verb exits 1 on `acked_late`, `overdue` and `never_notified` and takes `-max-ack` as a bound; `-db` and `-postgres-env` (a variable holding the DSN) read every run, or `-run`, from a store in the full `bide` command, and the audit-only build reads exported files.
