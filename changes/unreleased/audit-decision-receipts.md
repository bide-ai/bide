`audit.DecisionReceipt` (format `bide.audit.decision-receipt.v1`, `audit.ReceiptFormat`) is the signed decision an approver keeps, with the journal record's name and position and when it was recorded, and no key material: `NewDecisionReceipt` makes one, `Verify` checks it on its own, and `audit.CheckReceipt` decides whether the signed tree heads of the journal commit to the decision, as `anchored`, `redacted (anchored)`, `pending`, `receipt not matched by the journal`, `decision deleted after signing` (only for a receipt with an anchoring proof, `AnchorReceipt`) or `unchecked`, by the receipt's journal position and never by time. `bide audit verify-receipts` runs it from files and, with `-update`, stores the anchoring proof in each receipt, and `bide approve` writes a receipt for each signed decision (`-receipt`) and checks one with `-verify-receipt`.
