Model 15 (`spec/tla/identity`) gains a CA tier: the leaf's issuing CA is certified by a root that publishes its own, slower CRL series, may withhold a CRL and may revoke the CA with a past effective time, and the count and the pre-execution check read each tier at its own status age (`MaxAge` and `ExecAge` for the leaf's issuer, `MaxAgeCA` and `ExecAgeCA` for the CA, as the two-tier `ExecStatusAgeFor` design has it). `NoExecAfterKnownRevocation` is checked per tier (`NoExecAfterLeafTierRevocation`, `NoExecAfterCATierRevocation`) by `id-ca-tier`, and three counterexample configurations fail with the property they guard: a check that reads only the leaf tier, a count that ignores the root, and the leaf aged by the CA tier's longer age. A final verdict also reads the CA tier (a root CRL a window past the decision and none that lists the CA; `id-final-ca`, `final-no-ca-tier`), and the tally recheck covers the root archive. The nightly identity state counts in the model 15 README are re-measured.
