Section: Added

Package `approvals` is an `http.Handler` an application mounts to take approvers' signed decisions over HTTP: `GET /pending` shows a gated call from the journal (tool, arguments, the arguments' SHA-256, the gate), and `POST /decisions` verifies a signed decision against the recorded call and the approver's enrolled key, then records it through the application's own journal, so an `audit.AuditedStore` signs it as it is written, and answers the gate's standing (from the gate's recorded tally once it has settled, with separation of duties honored), whether the decision counts, and its receipt. `WithGatePolicy` is required and gives the handler each tool's live policy; the handler evaluates the gate with the new `agent.EvaluateGate`, the function the agent's own gate evaluates with (a gate the gate would refuse is state `error` and `409 gate_misconfigured`, a one-decision gate is state `single`), so a policy changed after the first pause (`ExcludePrincipal` turned on) is honored and the new approvals are not refused as `not_waiting`. A gate that asks again (its recorded tally passed on decisions of an earlier format, or without excluding a requester the policy now excludes) is `waiting`, not decided. Every response sent without reading a request body (a refusal, a 404 or 405, a GET that carries one) closes the connection instead of waiting for the body, and every path, those that route to nothing included, is rate limited; IPv6 clients are limited by their /64; the authorization hook's two calls for a POST are told apart by `Access.Phase`. A POST is authorized from its headers before its body is read, the body is read in a bounded pool under a deadline, and only then does the request take a slot for work on the journal; requests are strict JSON, size-bounded, rate limited per client and overall, and capped in concurrent work; `WithAuth` takes a mutual-TLS or identity check; a replay is a no-op; `OnRecorded` runs once for the request that wrote the decision, to re-run the run. It adds `agent.DecisionStep` (the journal name of a decision), `agent.RootRunID`, and `agent.ReportWritten` (an option for `SubmitDecision` that says whether this call's insert stored the record, so of concurrent identical submissions exactly one acts). Single-approver gates and plan flow node gates are refused.
