Section: Added

Approval deadlines: `agent.ApprovalPolicy.Timeout` (and `timeout` in a plan config's `approval` block) gives a gate a deadline, measured from the gate's recorded request and recorded once as `approval-deadline:<call>` (`agent.ApprovalDeadline`, `agent.RecordedDeadline`), so a redeploy does not move it. A gate past its deadline with no terminal count records its expiry as its outcome, insert-once under the name a passing tally or a single decision would take (`ApprovalTally.Expired`, `ExpiresAt`), so an expiry and a passing tally cannot both be recorded: the call never runs (the model reads `agent.ExpiredResult`; a plan flow returns `plan.ErrApprovalExpired`, which wraps `plan.ErrApprovalDenied`), a decision recorded after the deadline never counts (`agent.ReasonLate`), and `SubmitDecision`, `Approve`, the approvals endpoint (state `expired`, `expires_at`) and `bide approve` refuse a decision past it (`agent.ErrApprovalExpired`). `agent.EvaluateGate` reports `Expired` and `Deadline`. The notifier sends `approval_expired` and closes the pending approval with the reason `expired`. A decision written before a plan flow gate's request no longer blocks a single-approval gate: `Approve` records the real decision under the gate's second name, which the gate honours. Model 1b gains the expiry (`ExpiredNeverRuns`, `LateNeverCounts`, `ExpiryAfterDeadline`, `OutcomeOnce`, configurations `ap-deadline-m-cross` and `ap-deadline-one-cross`, regression `ap-late-counts`). Journal format: one new record name, and the tally and policy encodings gain optional fields, with no format version change before 1.0.
