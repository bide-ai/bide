`bide approve` records your signed approval, or with `-reject` a rejection, of a call that waits at an approval gate: it reads the run, tool, arguments and gate from the journal (SQLite `-db`, or Postgres by `-postgres-env`), shows the SHA-256 of the arguments the signature binds, asks you to confirm, signs with your Ed25519 key and records the decision with `agent.SubmitDecision` (`agent.Approve` for a single-approver gate, unsigned). It refuses a call with no waiting gate, a gate already decided, an approver not in the policy, an approver who already decided, and on Unix a key file readable by group or others; an m-of-n decision requires `-approver-keys` (the enrolled public keys, the file `bide view` reads), refuses a key that is not the one enrolled and verifies the tally, and `-unverified` opts out with a warning and an unverified tally. `bide approve -list` lists the waiting calls with the command that decides each, and `bide keygen` creates an approver key pair (mode 0600, never overwritten; `-alg` picks `ed25519`, `ml-dsa-65` or the `ed25519+ml-dsa-65` hybrid) and prints the public key policies and verifiers read. Both commands hold the key as an `audit.Signer`.
