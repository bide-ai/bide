The viewer and the read-only builder tools show approval deadlines and separation of duties. `bide view` shows a waiting gate's deadline and the time left, an expired gate as `expired` with its deadline, and a decision written after the deadline as not counted (`Gate.ExpiresAt`, `Timeout`, `Expired`, `Overdue`, `Due`; `runview.MarkDeadlines`); the Needs attention list puts gates with a deadline first, nearest first, flags one due within the hour, and marks a gate past its deadline `OVERDUE` with no approve command, since it refuses decisions; `bide approve -list` shows the deadline too. `bide view -config`, `plan`'s `RenderMermaid` (which now labels a gated node with its gate) and `bide check -diff` show each gate's `timeout` and `exclude_principal` next to its need and approvers (`plan.TopologyGate.TimeoutText`), and `-diff` classifies a timeout change: adding or shortening one is strengthened (the gate fails closed sooner), removing or lengthening it is weakened (a run can wait longer, and a late decision counts), and `-fail-on-weaken` exits 1 on the latter.
