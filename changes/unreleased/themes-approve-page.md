Section: Changed

The approve page draws from the shipped themes, inside its pinned bundle: light and dark by the system's color scheme, high-contrast when the system asks for more contrast (prefers-contrast: more), and the system's colors under forced colors, instead of two hand-written palettes that never saw a contrast check. The page takes no theme from a script, a URL, config.json or its host (a test holds that), a control's edge reaches 3:1, text 4.5:1, a disabled button has a dashed edge as well as its color, and the focus ring is drawn. app.css, and so index.html (which carries its integrity hash) and approve-page.SHA256SUMS, change in this release and are about 5 KB larger: a deployment that pins the bundle by hash re-verifies the release's approve-page.SHA256SUMS and updates its pin, as for any change to the page; the page's behaviour, the script and its protocol are unchanged.
