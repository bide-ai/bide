Section: Changed

Upgrading: the approve page's SHA256SUMS and the Subresource Integrity hash in its index.html change in this release. Anyone who pins the approve page by hash, or hosts it on their own origin, must update the pin from this release's signed approve-page.SHA256SUMS (verify it as before) and redeploy the new bundle; a copy built earlier no longer matches. app.css is about 5 KB larger (1.5 KB to 6.9 KB); the script, the protocol and the page's behaviour are unchanged.

The approve page draws from the shipped themes, inside its pinned bundle: light and dark by the system's color scheme, high-contrast when the system asks for more contrast (prefers-contrast: more), and the system's colors under forced colors, instead of two hand-written palettes that never saw a contrast check. The page takes no theme from a script, a URL, config.json or its host (a test holds that, and another holds the bundle's hashes in a committed golden, so a later change to a shipped theme is a visible change to the signed bundle). Text is 4.5:1, a control's edge 3:1, a disabled button has a dashed edge as well as its color, and the focus ring is drawn. REJECT now differs from Approve in shape (a heavy double edge against a thin solid one), in every mode: before this release the two differed by color and by their words only, and under forced colors, where both take the system's button colors, by their words alone.
