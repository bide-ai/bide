`bide view` has a Needs attention list at `/attention` (and `/attention.json`), linked from the run list's headline and its "need a person" count: every run that is waiting for approval (the call, the gate's k of n, who approved and who is pending, how long it has waited), halted with an unknown outcome (with the `agent.ResolveHalt` call), or aborted, newest first, and, from `-db` or `-postgres`, whether the notifier told someone and the sink acknowledged it, plus runs whose recovery keeps failing (read from the notification trail in the same database). The page reloads itself in live mode and works without JavaScript; `internal/runview` has the `Options.Events`, `Options.ApproveCommand` and `Options.Now` fields and `Stores.Events`. The run list is ALL CLEAR only when the notification trail was read for every run and none has a failing recovery; without the trail it says "nothing in the journals needs a person" and that recovery failures are not visible. `bide sample` now seeds a notification trail (`sample.WriteNotifyTrail`) with acknowledged, failing and overdue examples. A page that did not read everything is never clear: a store that cannot list its runs (`CANNOT LIST`), a failed listing or run, runs past the run list's 5,000 cap, or a filter give a headline that says what was not read, and the JSON of both lists carries `status`, `unread`, `truncated` and `listable`. The Needs attention list reads only runs without `run:complete` (no cap), matches a sub-run's notification in its root run's trail, and both lists are cached for two seconds. The list shows the `bide approve` command for each pending approval (quoted, with a fallback for an unsafe id), and `bide view` takes `-postgres-env <VAR>` to read the DSN from the environment.
