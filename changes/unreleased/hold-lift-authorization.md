A legal hold's lift can need a second person: `bide journal hold authorize` has a second person sign, with a key of their own (a key file, or with the new `bide-hsm journal` a key in an HSM or KMS), their consent to lift one hold, bound to the hold as it stood (matter, runs, tree setting), the placement's trail position and leaf hash, the deployment and a validity; `bide journal hold lift -authorization FILE -authorizer-pubkey KEY -require-authorization` checks it before anything is written and embeds it in the lift's signed trail record (`audit.LiftLegalHold` takes `audit.WithLiftAuthorization`, `WithLiftAuthorizers` and `RequireLiftAuthorization`; `audit.NewLiftAuthorization` and `audit.LiftAuthorization` make and carry it; a refusal is `audit.ErrLiftUnauthorized`). The authorizer's key must not be the key that signs the trail or share a component with it (a hybrid key's Ed25519 half is the same key holder). `bide audit redactions` checks the embedded authorization offline: a bad signature, another hold or placement, a validity lapsed at the lift, the lifter's own key, or a key that is not one of the `-authorizer-pubkey` keys is the new finding `LIFT_UNAUTHORIZED`, and `-require-lift-authorization` makes a lift with none one too. `bide-hsm journal` is `bide journal` with its signing keys named by KMS or PKCS#11 reference. Placing a hold needs no second person.
