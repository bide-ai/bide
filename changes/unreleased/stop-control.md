The control run of the kill switch (`agent.StopControl`, `StopPolicy`, `StopRecord`): stop, lift, policy-transition and mode records under the reserved run ID `@stop`, which a recovery pass does not drive and no user run may use. The mode is each process's configuration: basic (no policy, the default) trusts the store, so anyone with store access sets and lifts and the records are plain; audited (a policy) counts only records signed by approvers of the policy in force at their position, lifts by a quorum of distinct lifters (one not the setter, configurable) and ignores plain and unsigned records. No store record can switch a process's mode or make it fail: mode records are evidence, and `StopState` lists what it rejected. The deployment's root policy, which carries a deployment ID that every signed record repeats (so records do not replay across deployments), is the trust anchor, and signed policy transitions approved by a quorum of the policy in force carry later ones; a process pins the root policy or a later one the chain reaches and walks forward from the root, so a rotation or an offboarding never drops a stop, and a chain it cannot walk (a pin no stored root reaches, a fork) makes the stops unknowable instead of silently dropping them, while a forged record in an audited deployment is only rejected; a basic process on an audited deployment does not lift a signed stop on a plain lift; `ChangeMode` to audited adopts the stops that are active in the basic view. every process declares its mode (`WithStopMode`), the chain is checked for forks and rollbacks (`ErrConfig`), and a control that cannot judge which stops are in force (a basic process on an audited deployment, an audited process whose pin the chain does not reach) reports `ErrStopsUnknowable` for the engine to fail closed on; `StopControl.State` folds the log into the effective stops; nothing in the engine reads it yet, so no run is stopped by this change. Model 16 (`spec/tla/stop`) checks the fold and a call's read of it, with eight counterexample configurations.
