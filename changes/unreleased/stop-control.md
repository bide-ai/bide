The control run of the kill switch (`agent.StopControl`, `StopPolicy`, `StopRecord`): stop, lift, policy-transition and mode records under the reserved run ID `@stop`, which a recovery pass does not drive and no user run may use. The mode is each process's configuration: basic (no policy, the default) trusts the store, so anyone with store access sets and lifts and the records are plain; audited (a policy) counts only records signed by approvers of the policy in force at their position, lifts by a quorum of distinct lifters (one not the setter, configurable) and ignores plain and unsigned records. No store record can switch a process's mode or make it fail: mode records are evidence, and `StopState` lists what it rejected. The policy a process loads is its trust root, and signed policy transitions approved by a quorum of the policy in force carry later ones, so a rotation or an offboarding never drops a stop; `ChangeMode` to audited adopts the stops that are active in the basic view. `StopControl.State` folds the log into the effective stops; nothing in the engine reads it yet, so no run is stopped by this change. Model 16 (`spec/tla/stop`) checks the fold and a call's read of it, with eight counterexample configurations.
