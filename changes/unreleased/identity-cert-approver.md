Section: Added

The counting rule for certificate-bound approvers in the planned approver identity feature (build step 1; nothing wires it into a gate yet, so no existing policy changes). `agent.CertDecisionVerifier` is an optional extension of `ApproverVerifier`: `TallyApprovals` checks a decision by the kind of the approver's verifier, never by what the record carries, and gives the verifier the whole journal and the record's position in it. `audit.CertApprover` implements it: a decision counts when it carries a chain and is signed over the `bide.approval.v4` bytes, a signed head of the run covers it (the smallest covering head fixes the time it became provable, and heads that contradict each other refuse it), the chain is accepted for the approver at that time, and revocation status allows it. The seat is the verified identity (`ident:` and the canonical identifier), one leaf key fills one seat, and separation of duties excludes a requester by identifier. A `DecisionCheck` of such a decision carries the finding: identity, tier, basis, status time and when it became provable. New `agent.ReasonNoCoveringHead` and `ReasonHeadTimes`.
