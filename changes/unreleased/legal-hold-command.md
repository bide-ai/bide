Section: Added

Added `bide journal hold place|lift|list` and the signed record of legal holds: `audit.PlaceLegalHold` places a hold in the store and then records it in the redaction trail (signed with the redaction key, bound to its position, with the matter, the runs, the reason, the ticket and who), and `audit.LiftLegalHold` records the lift first and then lifts the hold, so a hold is never lifted without evidence and a failure to record never leaves a run unprotected. `bide journal redact` refuses a held run and its dry run names the hold. `bide audit redactions` lists the holds the trail shows in force, compares them with the database's (`HOLD_NOT_IN_STORE` for one lifted by hand, `HOLD_NOT_IN_TRAIL` for one placed by hand, `HOLD_DIFFERS`: other runs, tree setting or matter), reports a lift or placement the trail does not match (`HOLD_UNMATCHED`), and reports an erasure that began while the trail showed a hold over the run (`REDACTED_UNDER_HOLD`: when the pass replaced records, or has no end record to say it did not); on Postgres the reading role needs `SELECT` on the holds table. Two operators placing or lifting the same hold at once leave one record, not a duplicate that the audit would report as `HOLD_UNMATCHED`. Placing a hold again records one whose record failed, and lifting again finishes a lift whose store step failed, with no second record; a hold lifted by hand can be recorded as lifted.
