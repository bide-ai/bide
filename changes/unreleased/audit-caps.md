`bide audit caps` (experimental) audits the amount-cap ledgers of a store, read-only. Each ledger run must read under the ledger's own rules, and its window total must be the sum of its live reserves. Its slots must agree with the `cap-reserve` records of the runs that made the calls (a deleted slot, a changed slot, a slot no run recorded) and with each attempt's `cap-intent`, which must fit the call's own time (a planted intent that moves a slot to another window fails) and the rule's index run. A release of a call whose tool produced a result fails, and any other release must be explained by the loop's `cap-release:` record or by the attempt's void (without either it fails when anchored and warns when not). With `-sth` and `-pubkey` the ledgers and the runs' cap records must be what the newest signed heads commit to, and entries after them fail as not attested; with `-unanchored` the report says first that whoever can write the store can delete a slot or append a release undetected. It lists the slots counted over the limit under a waiver and the reconcile exposure, and `-namespace`, `-rule`, `-window` and `-run` narrow the audit and list what they left unchecked. `bide sample` now writes a small cap ledger (`bide audit caps -db sample.db -unanchored`). The amount caps guide gains a worked configuration and the cost of a ledger on an `AuditedStore`. See docs/guides/amount-caps.md.
