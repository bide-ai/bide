`bide audit caps` (experimental) audits the amount-cap ledgers of a store, read-only: each ledger run must read under the ledger's own rules, its window total must be the sum of its live reserves, and its slots must agree with the `cap-reserve` records of the runs that made the calls (a deleted slot, a changed slot, a slot no run recorded). With `-sth` and `-pubkey` the ledger must be what the newest signed head commits to, and entries after it fail as not attested; with `-unanchored` the report says first that whoever can write the store can delete a slot or append a release undetected. It lists the slots counted over the limit under a waiver and the reconcile exposure, and `-namespace`, `-rule`, `-window` and `-run` narrow the audit and list what they left unchecked. `bide sample` now writes a small cap ledger (`bide audit caps -db sample.db -unanchored`). The amount caps guide gains a worked configuration and the cost of a ledger on an `AuditedStore`. See docs/guides/amount-caps.md.
