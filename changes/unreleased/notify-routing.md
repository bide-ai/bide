The notifier routes events and escalates the ones nobody handles. `agent.WithNotifyReceiver` names additional receivers (each its own `Notifier`, typically its own webhook and relay), `WithNotifyRoute` sends an event kind to one (approvals to the approvers' channel, halts and recovery failures to on-call), and `WithNotifyApproverRoute` routes an approval by approver ID; a gate whose approvers map to several receivers is delivered to each under its own key (`to:<receiver>:<key>`), so one receiver's acknowledgement never stands for another's. `WithNotifyEscalation` gives `outcome_unknown`, `approval_pending` and `recovery_failing` a chain of up to four steps: when the event is not acknowledged by its deadline (an approval: not decided by it) the next receiver is told in an `escalated` notification (`agent.NotificationEscalated`, `Notification.Escalates`) with a deadline of its own, and the chain stops when the event stops being current or, for an event that is not an approval, is acknowledged; receivers already told get the all-clear. Routing lives in the engine and not in a fan-out `Notifier`, so every receiver has its own audited `observed`, `claimed` and `acked` records, retries and overdue in the run's one trail. The trail gains two additive `observed` fields, `receiver` (empty for the default receiver, so trails written without routing are unchanged) and `level`, the event kind `escalated` and the keys `to:<receiver>:<key>` and `escalated:<level>:<key>`; `NotifyEvent.Receiver`, `NotifyEvent.Level` and `NotifyState.ByReceiver` read them. The webhook payload gains `receiver` and `escalates` (null on every other kind). TLA+ model 14 gains routing (two receivers of one gate) and escalation (`Levels`, `EscMode`) with their safety and liveness properties, and regression configurations for a shared acknowledgement, an early step, a skipped step, a step after an acknowledgement, no escalation and a step that outlives its event.
