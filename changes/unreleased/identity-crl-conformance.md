Conformance captures of two-tier approver PKIs for the planned certificate-bound approver identity status rule, with true delta CRLs: EJBCA Community 9.6.3 (an RSA-3072 root and an ECDSA P-256 issuing CA) and Microsoft AD CS on Windows Server 2025 (an RSA-3072 standalone root and standalone subordinate on two machines), captured by the manually dispatched workflow `crl-fixtures.yml`, checked in under `audit/testdata/ejbca` and `audit/testdata/adcs`, and exercised offline by tests that run `VerifyChain` and the status rule on the real certificates and CRLs, including the base and delta pairing, cumulative deltas, AD CS's ten minute back-dating of `thisUpdate` and `notBefore`, key compromise, supersession and hold release.
