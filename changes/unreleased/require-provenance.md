Section: Added

`agent.TaintPolicy.RequireProvenance` (experimental) makes the taint policy refuse a tool that states no provenance: a tool must declare `Untrusted` or the new `agent.Trusted(reason)` (the tool's results are the host's own; the reason is required, goes in the declarations digest, and writes no mark; `ToolSpec.Trusted` is the same declaration), else `agent.New` returns `ErrConfig` naming the tool. A new `Run`, `Send` or `SendOnce` must state `WithUntrustedInput` or `WithTrustedInput`, else `ErrConfig` at its first drive with nothing written (a sub-agent's run and a resume are not asked). The declarations document gains `require_provenance` and `trusted`; `bide doctor -diff` reports turning the requirement off, or replacing `Untrusted` by `Trusted`, as WEAKENED, and `-declarations` lists a tool with no provenance when the policy does not require one. The digest of a policy that does not set it is unchanged. The `bide init -agent` scaffold sets it. `Trusted`, `ToolSpec.Trusted` and `RequireProvenance` are experimental. See docs/guides/untrusted-content.md.
