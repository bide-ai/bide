The `bide` command colors its text output on a terminal, for a reader who scans it. `bide check` marks each error with a red ✗, splits a violated assertion over the assertion, its reason and its counterexample path (in red, with the node it reaches in bold), so each line fits the terminal, colors risk classes as the risk map does (side effect red, read-only and idempotent green, an approval gate amber) and the verdict counts, and marks a config whose assertions all hold with a green ✓; `bide check -diff -check` colors added lines green, removed red and changed amber, and calls out a weakened approval gate first, in bold red, with the summary's counts and digest on lines of their own. `bide audit` marks `PASS`/`OK` lines with a green ✓ and `FAIL` lines with a red ✗, and a verify verb ends with a `VERIFIED` (green, with the signed tree's size and Merkle root dimmed) or `FAILED` (red) banner; the note `verify-evidence` puts on every approver decision line is said once, before the banner. `bide demo` gives each step its color (the crash red, the halt amber, the resolution blue, the proofs green) and now names the halt and the resolution in words ("halted: outcome unknown", "resolved with ResolveHalt"); `bide view` and `bide init` print the address to open and the next commands in bold cyan. Status is never carried by color alone: the words stay, and a mark is added beside them. Written to a file or a pipe, and under `bide audit -json`, the output is the plain text it was (golden tests pin it byte for byte). `init`, `demo`, `view`, `check` and `audit` take `-color=auto|always|never` (default `auto`); `auto` colors a terminal only, not with `NO_COLOR` set to any value or `TERM=dumb`, and on Windows only a terminal known to read ANSI escapes (Windows Terminal, ConEmu, ANSICON, VS Code, or a `TERM` such as `xterm-256color`). The styles are package `internal/term`, standard library only, so `bide audit` and the `bide_audit_only` build stay core-only. Help (`-h`) is unchanged. Screenshots in the [run viewer](docs/guides/run-viewer.md#color) and [audit](docs/guides/audit.md#cli-reference-bide-audit) guides, recorded by `assets/demo/cli.sh`; `assets/crash-demo.gif` is re-recorded in color.
