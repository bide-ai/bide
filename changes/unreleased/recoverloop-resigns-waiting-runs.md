`agent.RecoverLoop` and `agent.Recover` re-sign a run whose drive ended waiting on an approval or a halt resolution, including each of several joined pauses from parallel sub-agents (`agent.Reanchorer`, implemented by `audit.AuditedStore`, found through `Unwrap`; `agent.WithReanchor` names another function or turns it off; `agent.WithReanchorTimeout`, half the lease TTL by default, bounds each call so a hung anchor cannot hold a lease and a slot), so a decision another process wrote straight into the database is under a signed tree head within one pass instead of at the application's next write to the run. `audit.CheckDecisionCoverage` and `bide audit verify-decision-coverage` check a journal export against signed heads and flag a decision no head covers past a bound stated in journal positions (`-max-records`) or, as the weaker basis, age (`-max-age`, under which a decision with no recorded time is over the bound); a head the log key signed for the run that does not match the export is divergent and fails the check, so a deleted or truncated decision cannot pass.
