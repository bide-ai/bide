Section: Added

Formal model 15 (`spec/tla/identity`) checks the approver-identity design's counting rule before its Go code lands: certificates with a holder, an identifier, a validity interval and a revocation effective time, a CRL series with numbers, signed journal heads, and decisions that carry a certificate, under a CA that may mis-issue, a CRL that may be withheld or rolled back, a head stamped out of order, and a decision with an earlier `decided_at`. It checks `IdentityBound`, `RevocationSound`, `FailClosed`, `NoBackdate`, `SeatPerPerson`, `SeatPerIdentifier`, `TerminalStable`, `StaleNeverUncounts`, `NoExecAfterKnownRevocation`, `FinalNeedsCompleteSeries`, `AbsenceOnlyInWindow`, `TallyClean`, `DeadStaysOut` and `EventuallyCovered` (coverage by a head only, not that the gate then counts the decision), with a pruning CA, a gate crash, two seats under revocation and validity intervals that start late, and each rule has a counterexample configuration that switches it off and fails with the property it guards (no freshness rule, no non-decreasing head times, no name constraints, no series coverage, no gate `Anchor` step, no pre-execution status check, no `L(C)` rule on the live count or the check, no tally recheck, no dead set). Model 1b gains a certificate-bound approver (a leaf whose validity can turn false at any step), the pre-execution status check on every path to the tool (`NoExecAfterKnownRevocation`, with crashes, redeploys and recovery, also for a path that skips the check), the identity re-ask that lets a replacement decision under another leaf execute after a revocation held the call (`ReplacementSupersedes`), and the decision relation (approver, verdict, decision time, leaf: two leaves are two decisions, two chains for one leaf are one; `NoSquat`, `OneRecordPerDecision`, each of the four fields in the name required by a configuration); its existing configurations are unchanged apart from three constants that default to nothing. `modelsync` now holds `audit` `VerifyChain`, `EvaluateStatus` and `AuditedStore.Reanchor` and `agent.TallyApprovals` to model 15's map. The models run in their own CI shards.
