Formal model 15 (`spec/tla/identity`) checks the approver-identity design's counting rule before its Go code lands: certificates with a holder, an identifier, a validity interval and a revocation effective time, a CRL series with numbers, signed journal heads, and decisions that carry a certificate, under a CA that may mis-issue, a CRL that may be withheld or rolled back, a head stamped out of order, and a decision with an earlier `decided_at`. It checks `IdentityBound`, `RevocationSound`, `FailClosed`, `NoBackdate`, `SeatPerPerson`, `SeatPerIdentifier`, `TerminalStable`, `StaleNeverUncounts`, `NoExecAfterKnownRevocation`, `FinalNeedsCompleteSeries`, `AbsenceOnlyInWindow` and `NoStuckPause`, and each rule has a counterexample configuration that switches it off and fails with the property it guards (no freshness rule, no CRL number rule, no non-decreasing head times, no name constraints, no series coverage, no gate `Anchor` step, no pre-execution status check). Model 1b gains a certificate-bound approver (a leaf whose validity can turn false at any step), the pre-execution status check on every path to the tool (`NoExecAfterKnownRevocation`, with crashes, redeploys and recovery), and the decision relation (approver, verdict, decision time, leaf: two leaves are two decisions, two chains for one leaf are one; `NoSquat`, `OneRecordPerDecision`); its existing configurations are unchanged apart from three constants that default to nothing. `modelsync` now holds `audit` `VerifyChain`, `EvaluateStatus` and `AuditedStore.Reanchor` and `agent.TallyApprovals` to model 15's map. The models run in their own CI shard.
