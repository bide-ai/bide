Section: Added

A certificate-bound approver's decision can be taken over HTTP and checked before it is written. `agent.WithDecisionCheck` and `SubmitDecision` now verify a decision that carries a certificate chain through the approver's verifier (`agent.CertIntakeVerifier`, implemented by `audit.CertApprover.VerifyCertIntake`: the chain accepted for the approver at the time of the write, the `bide.approval.v4` signature under the leaf's key, status usable now) instead of refusing it, and write nothing on any failure; status that is missing or stale is the new `agent.ErrStatusUnavailable`. The approvals intake takes the chain in the request (`cert`, a base64 DER array, leaf first, at most 8 certificates of 16 KiB, bounded before any parsing), answers 503 `status_unavailable` with `Retry-After` and writes nothing when status cannot be judged, 422 for an invalid or revoked chain or a downgrade (a certificate-bound approver without a chain, a bare-key approver with one), answers a replay of a held decision from the record even during an outage, and anchors after the write. `counted: true` on a certificate-bound decision is not final: each decision carries `tier`, `provisional` and `basis`, the response carries `anchored`, `GET /pending` lists the identifier each certificate-bound approver's certificate must carry (`gate.identity`), and receipts carry the chain. `agent.Journal.Reanchor` asks the store to publish a head.
