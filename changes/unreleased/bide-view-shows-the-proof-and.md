Section: Added

`bide view` shows the proof and the approvals, and gets its finishing touches. An approval gate's approvers are cards: initials, what they decided, whether their signature verified (and why not), the fingerprint of their key (with `-approver-keys`), and when they decided (signed) and when bide recorded it. With a signed head or an evidence package the run page draws a proof path: the last covered record's leaf hash, the sibling hash at each level of its RFC 6962 audit path and the signed root, built from the proof the audit package verified (the page re-climbs the path for the intermediate hashes and draws nothing if it does not end at the root). A light and dark toggle (remembered in local storage, guarded for blocked storage), keyboard shortcuts (`j` and `k` through the records or runs, `/` to filter, `t`, `s`, `?` for help), a filter box on the timeline, a tab icon colored by the verdict and a title per page (`VERIFIED · refund-42 · bide view`), empty states for a list or filter with nothing, and a loading bar while a live page fetches. **Share this run** saves a 1200 by 630 PNG of the verdict, key numbers, graph or proof path, and proof line, drawn in the browser from the page with nothing sent anywhere. The pages still ask for no other host and carry no inline script or style.
