Section: Changed

A kill switch stop is now named by its scope, its compensation and a generation (`@stop/set/<scope key>/<generation>`, the generation in the signed record as `gen`) instead of by its content, and the insert is once-only, so two operators who set the same scope at the same instant can no longer leave it in force twice: the loser of the insert reads the control run again, finds the winner's stop in force, and writes nothing. `StopControl.SetStop` returns the stop in force with an `*AlreadyStoppedError` (`errors.Is(err, ErrAlreadyStopped)`) when its scope and compensation are already stopped, and `ErrStopContended` after repeated lost inserts; `bide stop` reports "already stopped" and exits 0 as before. A writer that squats or leaves a gap in the generations cannot block a stop: the next generation is one more than the greatest name present. Records written before generations keep their old names and are read as before; the fold is unchanged. Model 16 gains the generation rule (`SetInsertable`, the invariant `OneActivePerScope`, the configs `stop-same-scope` and `stop-same-scope-reach` and the regress `no-generation`). Guide: docs/guides/kill-switch.md.
