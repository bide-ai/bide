A retention and archiving guide, `docs/guides/retention.md`, for the people who set a bank's retention policy and the operators who carry it out: the questions a policy has to answer, what each artifact is (journals, evidence packages, signed tree heads and anchor entries, decision receipts, notification trails including the open trail's segments, seals, checkpoints and yearly indexes, relay state, keys) and which of them to keep, how to archive them so `bide audit` still verifies them offline years later (a manifest, the pinned verifier and the formats it reads, what each verb needs), how to rotate and retire keys without breaking old verification, what deleting does against the position-based detection of deletion (checked against a real journal: whole runs can be removed consistently, records and open-trail segments cannot, and bide ships no redaction), and storage on write-once media. It names no regulation as satisfied, and points to the approver-identity design (#302) for certificate and revocation-list archiving, which is not implemented. Linked from the trust pages, the docs index and the docs-site sidebar.
