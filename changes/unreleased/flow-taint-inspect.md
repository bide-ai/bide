`plan.ConfigReport.Taint` (a `plan.NodeTaint` for each node) is the static view of flow taint: which declared sources may run before a node, what the flow's taint policy would do to it (`escalates`, `refused`, `exempt`, or `reads untrusted` when the policy is not given), and what the view cannot know. `plan.InspectTaintPolicy` gives `InspectConfig` the policy (the zero `agent.TaintPolicy` states there is none); `InspectConfigDescribed` reads the description's. `bide check` prints a taint section, or one line "taint: not known without -blocks", and `bide check -diff` reports a side-effecting node that newly reads a source (listed when the policy gates it or it is exempt, weakened as `UNTRUSTED EXPOSURE WIDENED` when the policy is known to be absent, not certified when the policy is not given) and a source a node no longer declares. Experimental.
