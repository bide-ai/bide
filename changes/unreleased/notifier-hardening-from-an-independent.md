Hardening from an independent review: the overdue notice is derived from the trail and sent inside the round that holds the event's claim (so it survives a restart and a failed first delivery); every `Notify` call is bounded by a quarter of the claim and rounds of different events run concurrently, so one hung sink does not stall the rest, and `RecoverLoop` returns on cancel; another holder's claim counts as live for a skew margin past its `until`, and a new round is paced by the trail, so the claims of an undelivered event stay near four an hour whatever the number of sweeps; a decided gate (single decision, tally, tool or node result) is cleared, not reported; `NotifyPending` also finds aborted runs; any pause closes a recovery failure episode, a run no `Resumer` drives alerts, and a shutdown's cancellation does not; `recovery_failing` carries a class, never the Resumer's error text; an idle notifier holds no goroutine; `FlushNotifications` returns what it could not deliver; a plan flow keeps one notifier per store. A peer sends the overdue notice, under a short claim of its own, when the holder that was to send it may be gone, and a round's budget counts its backoff and acknowledgement retries so it ends inside its claim.
