A gate that asks again over a recorded tally it does not act on (one that predates `bide.approval.v3`, or one that passed without excluding the requester under a policy that now excludes it) records a re-ask note, `approval-pending:<id>:v3` or `:sod` (`agent.ApprovalReaskStep`): an `agent.ApprovalWait` with the policy it asks under, why (`Reask`) and the tally it asks over (`Supersedes`), written only if absent by every drive that pauses at the re-ask. `bide view`, `bide approve`, the approvals intake's note fallback, notifications and `audit.ApprovalEvidence` read the newest note (`agent.LatestPauseNote`), so after a redeploy adds `ExcludePrincipal` and a crash leaves a passing tally under the old policy, the CLI and the viewer show the gate waiting for the re-approval and take the approvals it needs. A re-ask notifies as an event of its own (`agent.ApprovalReaskKey`, `NotifyApproval.Reask`, `reask` in the webhook payload), `audit.ApprovalVerdict` gains `Reask` and `ReaskedAt`, and `plan.Flow.Conform` accepts re-ask notes. A re-ask note counts only where the journal proves it (`agent.ValidReaskNote`: a recorded tally the gate would ask again over, which the note names), so a note pre-inserted by a journal writer without approver keys is ignored by every reader, and the notifier's catch-up scan, escalations and `agent.JournalNotifyEvents` (so `audit.VerifyNotifications`) keep the re-ask's own key and question. The record is additive: earlier readers ignore it, except an earlier `Conform`, which reports it as unexpected.
