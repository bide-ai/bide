`docs/guides/sso-certificates.md` and the `examples/sso-certs` module: a worked, tested recipe for short-lived approver certificates issued after a single sign-on login, with step-ca 0.30.2 as the reference CA (an OIDC provisioner and a certificate template that issue exactly what an `IdentityPolicy` requires, the lifetime and backdate rule, an operator JWK provisioner for revocation) and `scripts/renew.sh` for the approver. The module's test runs the recipe against pinned step-ca, step CLI and mock OIDC identity provider containers: the real login, the certificate's shape, a login without MFA or with a malformed person id refused by the template, `bide identity fetch-crl` and `bide identity check` over step-ca's CRL, a gated call approved with `bide approve -cert` and counted by a real `audit.CertApprover`, a revocation and the refused decision made after it. It runs in the Integration job with `BIDE_REQUIRE_STEPCA=1` and elsewhere only with `BIDE_TEST_STEPCA=1`; the guide states what the test does not cover (an HSM or KMS key, another identity provider, other CAs).
