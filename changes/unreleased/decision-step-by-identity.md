`agent.SubmitDecision` names a decision's journal step by the decision (call, approver, verdict, scheme and decided_at), never by its signature bytes, so re-signing and resubmitting one decision concurrently with a randomized scheme (ECDSA P-256 from a KMS or HSM) journals one record instead of two, and `agent.ReportWritten` reports exactly one write; `agent.DecisionStep` returns the new name. Journals written under the old signature-derived names read, count and verify unchanged, a resubmission of such a decision writes nothing more, and a record that is not the approver's valid decision never takes the name the real decision needs (it is then journaled under the signature-derived name). An unchecked resubmission of a decision recorded under the old name is still a no-op that reports no write, and `agent.HeldDecisionStep` finds the record that holds a decision wherever it is (`DecisionStep` is the name written first, not a lookup key).
