Section: Fixed

`agent.SubmitDecision` names a decision's journal step by the decision (call, approver, verdict, scheme and decided_at), never by its signature bytes, so re-signing and resubmitting one decision concurrently with a randomized scheme (ECDSA P-256 from a KMS or HSM) journals one record instead of two, and `agent.ReportWritten` reports exactly one write; `agent.DecisionStep` returns the new name. Journals written under the old signature-derived names read, count and verify unchanged, a resubmission of such a decision writes nothing more, and a record that is not the approver's valid decision never takes the name the real decision needs (it is then journaled under the signature-derived name). Behaviour change in plan flow gates: an approver's decision recorded before the gate's request under the old signature-derived name no longer blocks their identical decision after the pause; the fresh decision gets its own name and counts (one recorded before the request under the new name still reports ErrConfig and must be re-signed at a new decided_at).
