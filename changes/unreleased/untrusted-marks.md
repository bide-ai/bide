Section: Added

Untrusted-content marks (experimental, part of the design in `docs/design/untrusted-content-and-amount-caps.md`): `agent.Untrusted(source)` declares that a tool's results are untrusted content, and the engine records the source with every result (`Safety.Untrusted` of the result record, including denials, errors and a saga's failure record); `agent.WithUntrustedInput` and `agent.WithTrustedInput` state the provenance of a run's input in `run:start`'s settings, and a later drive that passes another is `ErrConfig`; `agent.RetrievalUntrusted` marks a retrieval layer's step; `agent.TaintExempt(reason)` is a Go-only exemption declaration. Marks are recorded with or without a taint policy and gate nothing yet. A tool that embeds or unwraps an `Untrusted` tool and does not carry the declaration is refused at registration (as for an approval gate, a timeout or caps), `Step` refuses a `Safety` that names an untrusted source, and a tool that declares `Untrusted` or `TaintExempt` is refused as a plan flow node until flows have a taint rule. Records without marks are byte for byte as before; older binaries ignore the new members.
