Section: Changed

**Breaking:** every signed m-of-n approval decision now carries two times, kept apart. `decided_at` is when the approver decided, by their clock, and is inside the signature: `ApprovalDecisionBytes` takes it (`ApprovalDecisionBytes(subject, approverID, approved, decidedAt)`), `Decision.DecidedAt` is required (`SubmitDecision` refuses a zero one with `ErrConfig`), and the signed encoding is now `bide.approval.v3` (`agent.DecisionFormat`), which appends `len32(decided_at) decided_at` after the decision byte (the m-of-n design note works one decision through to its exact bytes, pinned by `TestApprovalDecisionBytes_Vector`). It is signed in one form only, RFC 3339 in UTC to the millisecond with a `Z`, years 0001 to 9999 (`agent.FormatTimestamp`, 24 bytes, the form of `started_at`); a verifier parses it, formats it again and refuses the decision unless the two are byte for byte the same (`agent.ParseTimestamp`, `agent.ReasonDecidedAt`), so a decision whose time was changed after signing does not verify. **Decisions signed under the previous encoding (`bide.approval.v2`) no longer verify**: the gate and `audit.VerifyApprovals` ignore them with `agent.ReasonNoDecidedAt`, there is no fallback, and they must be signed again. **A run whose gate passed on v2 decisions before the upgrade, and whose call has not run, does not run it on resume**: the gate asks again (`*ApprovalPending` with `Reason` `agent.PendingReapproval`, its `Quorum.Supersedes` naming the old tally), and once approvers sign under v3 it records the new outcome under `agent.ApprovalRetallyStep`, which the gate, `audit.ApprovalEvidence`, `audit.VerifyApprovals` and `bide view` read in place of the old tally (`agent.RecordedTally`); a v2 denial stays final. Every journaled tally now names its format (`ApprovalTally.DecisionFormat`). `recorded_at` (`Record.RecordedAt`) is when `SubmitDecision` journaled the decision, by the submitting process's clock (`WithClock`, now also an `ApproveOption`, else the run clock on the context, else the wall clock; one with no canonical form is `ErrConfig`): that submitter's claim, not signed, anchored by the journal's Merkle trail once a signed head covers it. `ApprovalWait` (`approval-pending`) records when the run paused, and a plan flow gate's `ApprovalRequest` when it recorded the request: when approvers were asked. Both times are display only: `TallyApprovals` checks `decided_at` only as signed bytes and orders decisions by journal position, so the same decisions count the same whatever their times (`TestTally_IgnoresTimes`), and a decision signed a day ahead still counts (`TestApprovalTimes_FlagsNeverUncount`). A decision's journal name now hashes `decided_at` too, so a copy of a valid signature journaled first under another time cannot take the name the real decision needs, and an approver whose identical decision was recorded before a flow gate's request signs again at a new time and counts (the seat is no longer lost; `Flows.tla`'s `ADecide` and the new `flows/flow-gate-early` configuration, which replaces the `gate-early-stuck` limit); `WithDecisionCheck` reads, as the gate does, only decisions after a flow gate's request. `audit.ApprovalEvidence` includes the gate's `approval-pending` record (a `step` item) when the journal holds one, and `audit.VerifyApprovals` reports each counted decision's two times (`ApprovalVerdict.Times`, `AskedAt`, `HeadAt`) with sanity flags that never change the verdict (`audit.CheckDecisionTimes`, tolerance `DefaultDecisionTimeTolerance`, 5 minutes): `FlagDecidedAfterRecorded`, `FlagDecidedBeforeAsked`, `FlagAskedUnknown`, `FlagRecordedAtInvalid`, `FlagRecordedOutOfOrder` and `FlagRecordedAfterHead`. `bide audit verify-approvals` prints both times, the gap and a `note:` per flag, with `-decision-time-tolerance`, and prints any time that is not canonical quoted, so an unsigned field cannot add lines to its report; `bide view`'s approver cards show the times in place of the signed head's time, and only the counted decision's when an approver has several. `ApproveOption` is now an interface (`WithDecisionCheck` and `WithClock` implement it). An RFC 3161 trusted timestamp per decision is possible future work.
