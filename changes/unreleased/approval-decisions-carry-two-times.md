Section: Changed

**Breaking:** every signed m-of-n approval decision now carries two times, kept apart. `decided_at` is when the approver decided, by their clock, and is inside the signature: `ApprovalDecisionBytes` takes it (`ApprovalDecisionBytes(subject, approverID, approved, decidedAt)`), `Decision.DecidedAt` is required (`SubmitDecision` refuses a zero one with `ErrConfig`), and the signed encoding is now `bide.approval.v3`, which appends `len32(decided_at) decided_at` after the decision byte. It is signed in one form only, RFC 3339 in UTC to the millisecond with a `Z` (`agent.FormatTimestamp`, 24 bytes, the form of `started_at`); a verifier parses it, formats it again and refuses the decision unless the two are byte for byte the same (`agent.ParseTimestamp`, `agent.ReasonDecidedAt`), so a decision whose time was changed after signing does not verify. **Decisions signed under the previous encoding (`bide.approval.v2`) no longer verify**: the gate and `audit.VerifyApprovals` ignore them with `agent.ReasonNoDecidedAt`, there is no fallback, and they must be re-signed. `recorded_at` (`Record.RecordedAt`) is when `SubmitDecision` journaled the decision, by bide's clock (`WithClock`, now also an `ApproveOption`, else the run clock on the context, else the wall clock), anchored by the journal's Merkle trail; `ApprovalWait` (`approval-pending`) records when the run paused, and a plan flow gate's `ApprovalRequest` when it recorded the request: when approvers were asked. Both times are display only: `TallyApprovals` checks `decided_at` only as signed bytes and orders decisions by journal position, so the same decisions count the same whatever their times (`TestTally_IgnoresTimes`). A decision's journal name now hashes `decided_at` too, so a copy of a valid signature journaled first under another time cannot take the name the real decision needs. `audit.ApprovalEvidence` includes the gate's `approval-pending` record (a `step` item) when the journal holds one, and `audit.VerifyApprovals` reports each counted decision's two times (`ApprovalVerdict.Times`, `AskedAt`) with sanity flags that never change the verdict: a `decided_at` more than 5 minutes after `recorded_at` (`audit.FlagDecidedAfterRecorded`, `DefaultDecisionTimeTolerance`, `FlagDecisionTimes` for another tolerance) and a `decided_at` before the run asked (`audit.FlagDecidedBeforeAsked`; the ask is a flow gate's request time, else the pause's, and `audit.FlagAskedUnknown` when the evidence holds neither). A decision signed a day ahead still counts (`TestApprovalTimes_FlagsNeverUncount`). `TestApprovalDecisionBytes_Vector` pins the exact signed bytes of the worked example in the m-of-n design note. `bide audit verify-approvals` prints both times, the gap and a `note:` per flag, with `-decision-time-tolerance`, and `bide view`'s approver cards show them in place of the signed head's time. `ApproveOption` is now an interface (`WithDecisionCheck` and `WithClock` implement it). An RFC 3161 trusted timestamp per decision is possible future work.
