Section: Added

Added the signed acknowledgement of a planted redaction-trail record: `audit.AcknowledgeRecord` and `bide journal acknowledge -position N -reason ... -ticket ... -deployment ... -sign-key ... -head-out ...` append a record, signed with the redaction key and bound to its position, that names a leaf the tool did not write by its position and the hash of its bytes, with the reason, the ticket and who, and sign a new head. `bide audit redactions` then reports that leaf as acknowledged (a note, printed as INFO, that says who acknowledged it, the ticket, the time and the reason) instead of failing on its BAD_SIGNATURE, REORDERED or MALFORMED. It acknowledges only that leaf: the acknowledgement must come after it, a record the tool wrote or an erasure made by hand cannot be acknowledged, other bytes at that position fail again, and an unsigned or wrongly signed acknowledgement acknowledges nothing. `audit.CheckRedactionTrail` is the check `RedactJournal` makes before it appends, for the command.
