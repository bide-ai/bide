The certificate-bound decision format of the planned approver identity feature (build step 1; the gate does not use it yet, so no behaviour changes for bare-key approvers). `agent.ApprovalDecisionBytesCert` (`bide.approval.v4`) is the v3 layout plus the SHA-256 of the signer's leaf certificate (`agent.CertLeafHash`); `agent.Decision.Cert` and the record member `approver_cert` carry the leaf-first chain (at most 8 certificates of 16 KiB), `SubmitDecision` journals it and adds the leaf hash to the decision's step name, and `WithDecisionCheck` refuses a decision that carries one until its verifier exists. A bare-key verifier never counts a v4 signature. `audit.DecisionReceipt` gains `cert`; `Verify` refuses a receipt that carries a chain (`audit.ErrCertificateBound`), and the new `VerifyCertificate(ci, logKey)` checks the chain and identifier at the time of the head the receipt's own anchoring proof carries (`AnchorTime`), then the signature under the leaf's key. A decision's identity includes its leaf in `WithDecisionCheck` and `HeldDecisionStep`.
