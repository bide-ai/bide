The certificate-bound decision format of the planned approver identity feature (build step 1; the gate does not use it yet, so no behaviour changes for bare-key approvers). `agent.ApprovalDecisionBytesCert` (`bide.approval.v4`) is the v3 layout plus the SHA-256 of the signer's leaf certificate (`agent.CertLeafHash`); `agent.Decision.Cert` and the record member `approver_cert` carry the leaf-first chain (at most 8 certificates of 16 KiB), `SubmitDecision` journals it and adds the leaf hash to the decision's step name, and `WithDecisionCheck` refuses a decision that carries one until its verifier exists. A bare-key verifier never counts a v4 signature. `audit.DecisionReceipt` gains `cert`, `Verify` checks the v4 signature, and the new `VerifyCertificate` and `AnchorTime` check the chain and identifier at the time the decision became provable.
