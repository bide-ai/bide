Section: Added

Separation of duties for m-of-n approval gates: `agent.ApprovalPolicy.ExcludePrincipal` (a flow config's `approval: {..., exclude_principal: true}`) makes the gate never count a decision by the run's requester, the principal its `run:start` journals (`Identity.OnBehalfOf`), compared ignoring ASCII case, with `agent.ReasonRequester`; the tally records `ExcludePrincipal`, `Requester` and the requester in `Excluded`, and the gate refuses with `ErrConfig` a run with no requester, a requester that is not printable ASCII, or a policy whose `Need` is out of reach without the requester. A plan flow run now journals the principal of the identity its context carries (`agent.ContextWithIdentity`, new), and a later flow drive under another principal is `ErrConfig`. `audit.ApprovalEvidence` discloses the run's `run:start` for such a gate, `audit.VerifyApprovals` recounts with the same exclusion (`ApprovalVerdict.Requester`), `bide audit verify-approvals` and `bide view` take `-exclude-principal`, `bide view` marks the requester's card as excluded and a config diff reports dropping the exclusion as a weakened gate, the flow assertion `every path to X passes approval >= K of N excluding the requester` checks it on the graph, and model 1b checks `SeparationOfDuties`. New exported helpers: `agent.RecordedRequester`, `agent.IsRunStart`, `agent.ReasonRequester`, `agent.ReasonNoRequester`, `plan.TopologyGate.ExcludePrincipal`.
