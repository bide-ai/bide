Section: Added

Separation of duties for m-of-n approval gates: `agent.ApprovalPolicy.ExcludePrincipal` (a flow config's `approval: {..., exclude_principal: true}`) makes the gate never count a decision by a requester of the run, its journaled principal (`Identity.OnBehalfOf`) or that of any run above it (a sub-agent's, a delegation's, a flow started from a tool), compared ignoring ASCII case, with `agent.ReasonRequester`; the tally records `ExcludePrincipal`, `Requesters` and the excluded approvers in `Excluded`, and the gate refuses with `ErrConfig` a run with no requester or an ancestor that records none, a requester that is not printable ASCII, a principal journaled after the call's request (a run an earlier version started), or a policy whose `Need` is out of reach without the requesters. A run's first drive now records `run:principal` (`agent.RunPrincipal`: its principal and its ancestors', nothing of its input) before `run:start`, which also records the ancestors (`RunStart.Ancestors`); a plan flow run now journals the principal of the identity its context carries (`agent.ContextWithIdentity`, new), and a later flow drive under another principal is `ErrConfig` (a flow run recorded with none, as every earlier one was, keeps none). `audit.ApprovalEvidence` discloses the run's `run:principal` (not its `run:start`) for such a gate, `audit.VerifyApprovals` recounts with the same exclusion (`ApprovalVerdict.Requesters`), `bide audit verify-approvals` and `bide view` take `-exclude-principal`, `bide view` marks a requester's card as excluded and a config diff reports dropping the exclusion as a weakened gate, the flow assertion `every path to X passes approval >= K of N excluding the requester` checks it on the graph, model 1b checks `SeparationOfDuties` up a delegation chain, and `examples/approval/makerchecker` runs a maker-checker refund. New exported API: `agent.RunPrincipal`, `agent.RecordedPrincipal`, `agent.IsRunPrincipal`, `agent.ContextWithIdentity`, `agent.ReasonRequester`, `agent.ReasonNoRequester`, `plan.TopologyGate.ExcludePrincipal`.
