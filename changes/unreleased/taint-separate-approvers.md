`agent.TaintPolicy.SeparateApprovers` (experimental) makes a taint policy's escalation gate a second look by other people. On a call's escalation instance (`<call>#taint`, or `node:<name>:taint` in a plan flow) the approvers whose decisions the call's own gate counted do not count, the tally records them as `excluded_by_sibling`, and an instance left with too few approvers to reach the Gate's `Need` is denied instead of waiting. `agent.New` and `plan` `Build` are `ErrConfig` for a tool or node that can be escalated whose own gate is one decision or shares an approver ID with the policy's Gate, and for a policy that separates approvers with a `SingleApproval` or `Refuse`. The field is in the declarations digest and the `bide doctor` document only when set, and `bide doctor -diff` reports its removal as weakened. `audit.ApprovalEvidence` carries the own gate's tally with an instance's evidence, and `audit.VerifyApprovals` recounts it with the same exclusion under `agent.ApprovalPolicy.SeparatedFrom`; `bide audit verify-approvals` takes `-separate-from`. A policy digest changes only for a policy that sets the field.
