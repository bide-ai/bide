Section: Added

The notifier can now sign and anchor its notification trail as it is written: wrap the trail's event store in `audit.NewAuditedEventStore(store, signer, anchor)` (new) and each growth of a run's trail publishes a signed tree head (kind events, run ID the trail's name, the head `audit.SignNotifyTrail` signs) with the same `audit.Signer` and `audit.Anchor` as a journal, so HSM, KMS and P-256 signers work unchanged. Publishing is a background, best-effort side channel: a publish failure or a hung anchor never fails or delays a delivery or an acknowledgement (one attempt is abandoned after `WithTimeout`, default 3s, and a failure pauses publishing for `WithCooldown`, default 30s), failures go to `OnError`, heads only grow per trail, and `Flush`, `Reanchor` and `ReanchorPending` publish what an outage left out. `NotifyPending`, `RecoverLoop`, `Recover` and `FlushNotifications` sign again any trail (the open trail's segments and index and the digest's trail included) that grew past its last head through the new optional `agent.NotifyTrailAnchorer` (implemented by `audit.NotifyTrail`). `bide audit notifications` now takes any number of `-sth` heads per trail (each must verify and match a prefix; the newest is used), reports how many records follow the newest head and how long the oldest has gone unattested, and fails a gap older than the new `-max-unattested`; `audit.NotifyVerifyInput.MaxTailAge` and the `Heads`, `TailSince` and `TailAge` report fields are new. Model 14 is unchanged: anchoring is outside the delivery protocol.
